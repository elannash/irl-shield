# Complementarity protocol v2

The local OCR truth/union-leak instruments, actual area aggregation at ceiling
<=0.15, runtime guards and frozen GeoCLIP protocol drive policy selection. This
harness supplies complementary grounded findings, not a scalar reward. Utility
acceptance belongs to the owner's eye. No 1–10 acceptance bar, weighted privacy/
utility score, 600-item dataset, effort sweep, paid API or automatic promotion.

The authoritative design is `grading-architecture-CONSENSUS.md` plus the adopted
Sol recommendation §3 and §E, including the four conductor amendments. Durable
copies live under root's `.tmp/reboot-checkpoint-20261030/tmp-conductor-hackathon/research/`.
The original W12 pack with digest `474d059e...` is historical, ungraded and
superseded. **Never dispatch it.** Its synthetic control inspections are neither
natural primary labels nor validated strong-reference judgments.

## Inputs and the pilot boundary

The pilot cannot start until root supplies at least one locally qualified W1
challenger and exact rendered-output receipts. `request` writes a 12-slot
MISSING_INPUTS skeleton, not cards. With no qualifying inputs `seal` fails before
creating a public directory. No actual pilot or grader invocation is authorized
by this module. Root/Opus own dispatch, independent auditing and local positive
checking. The code contains no network, model, OCR, GPU or render launcher.

Pilot: 12 matched **source-event** comparisons across all seven natural tune
clips, at most two per clip: 24 output items and four controls. Variants, crops,
frames, repeated judgments and the two policies are not independent events.
Each card has up to eight time-ordered PNG frames from a two-second interval and
up to two processed-output crops. Both policies use identical times/crop rules.
Root freezes representative and targeted-failure strata before seeing results;
seek four residual-risk types where real evidence exists, without assumed
positives. Sparse cards cannot establish continuous privacy or full flicker.
Local full-rate instruments and playback own temporal claims.

Each item binds source origin/event/clip/dataset/license, source/policy/output
hashes, a license/split provenance receipt, extraction receipt, and (for honest
items) a LOCALLY_QUALIFIED receipt. Qualification binds the exact source, policy
and output and records passing OCR/runtime/real-video/area guards, actual area
aggregation, 1–3 s buffer and frozen GeoCLIP non-regression. It references the
local instrument artifacts by absolute path/hash. These are trusted root-issued
receipts: hashing a supplied assertion does not independently establish its
measurement. Missing, mismatched or failed qualification prevents a pilot.

Every source/access check for the whole plan precedes any image hash/open.
Owner-derived imagery is forbidden externally in every form: raw, shielded or
cropped; `owner-real` aliases are rejected. Owner footage remains local-only.
Raw controls must be natural licensed fixture frames, never synthetic overlays.
Unknown licenses/splits fail. Spent-v1 is development-only, accepted exclusively
for the locked check with exact manifest SHA
`46a4461ca50ac54a4cac564ad81e1bba5c2d990b0cb9976eb473a82e5fcc3cad`.
A legacy path containing `fresh-heldout` is not a blanket bypass: that exact
spent dataset binding and locked phase are required. Fresh-v2 requires the final
frozen ONE_SHOT receipt, root authorization, exact dataset and policy binding.
This lane never opens v2 pixels or evaluates that future policy.

## Controls and first-pass observations

Four fixed, locally validated controls are required: known-readable licensed raw
leak; the same target genuinely removed; black/overblur utility failure; generic/
synthetic context without a grounded specific location. Control references are
private and must be supplied by root; no old inspection becomes a label. An
A/A utility tie and a reversed A/B consistency check consume the special strong
pool. They are not free additional calls. Reversed choices are compared by the
underlying item, not the presented A/B label. Uncertain comparisons cannot pass.

Per-card JSON records all five categories (text/sign, logo/storefront/landmark,
house number/address, plate, bystander face) as present, absent_in_sample or
uncertain. Ground present findings in a delivered frame ID/timestamp, normalized
bbox and exact string or recognizable cue. Severity 0–3 is ordinal, not a reward:
0 no cue; 1 generic context; 2 grounded protected content; 3 direct identifier.
No person identification; exposed faces are counted. Utility uses acceptable,
unacceptable or uncertain with concrete defect evidence. Unsupported place
guesses stay generic/uncertain. Missing attachments and timeouts never become
negative findings. Bound all fields, reject unknown/missing/duplicate keys or
categories, nonfinite values and mismatched digests/IDs. Limit visible output to
250 words without clamping hidden reasoning. Returned text stays inert JSON;
it never becomes a path, command, HTML or executable content.

## Blinding and sealing

Each fresh grader context receives only **one** neutral item directory: random
PNG names, items.json, rubric.md, response.schema.json and its own start token.
No source/arm names, private references, original footage, instrument outcomes,
other answers, repository or history. A special utility comparison can receive
its explicitly assigned two items only. Root must enforce attachment isolation;
a directory on a shared machine is not a sandbox. RGB PNG metadata is rejected;
there is no automatic image transformation or media build.

Public card digests cover the canonical card excluding its digest field plus
hashes of every allowlisted file. Extra files/symlinks/drift fail. The private
SEALED map stays outside public roots and is pinned by an independently recorded
map hash. Random card order and neutral IDs hide policy position. Exclusive
read-only files and hash receipts are tamper-evident, not OS immutability against
the owner. `begin` pins the local before-decision, code bytes, each actual model/
effort and a unique fresh context per item. `finish` validates and seals each
answer, start hash, attachment receipt and finish time. Completed text-only
answers cannot establish image capability. Actual licensed image-control receipt
is necessary before claiming capability; availability stays null in this lane.

**All actual first-pass answers**, including registered Luna shadows, must be
sealed before `reveal` reads a private map or any reference-assisted positive
check. Missing/timeouts may be explicitly sealed as uncertain; they cannot be
silently dropped. Drift and replay fail. Reveal emits per-grader control outcomes;
a failed control stops automatic use of that grader. It preserves every answer,
contradiction and readable/cue hit. Hits map to source event/clip, source/output/
policy hashes and actual source frames using floor plus clamp to the delivered
frame count: at 30 fps, 1.99 seconds maps to frame 59, never frame 60.

## Decisions, budgets and stops

Record OCR+area/runtime+GeoCLIP decisions before panel reveal. After all seals,
local reference-assisted checks bind to the unblind barrier and exact map hash.
Only a verified promotion/veto change or actual utility tie resolution counts.
Known OCR/GeoCLIP failure descriptions do not. New positive evidence must be
localized and locally verified; decisive unresolved evidence prevents promotion.
Luna is triage only, with no clearance or veto; consequential leads need strong
confirmation. Utility ties need owner acceptance and passing pairwise controls.
The report preserves all findings even when recurring grading stops.

After 12 interpretable comparisons: zero verified changes stops recurring work
and retains the final audit; one permits only four more; continuation requires
a second useful change on a different clip. Two changes on different clips earn
the conditional Luna check. Missing/uninterpretable comparisons stay uncertain.
Stop at pilot 2 h, quota failure, uninterpretable controls or projected missed
freeze. One prompt revision on the 24 pilot items + four controls is permitted,
then one locked spent-v1 check of 20 verified positive and 20 negative DISTINCT
events. Missing/unresolved data skips it. Require zero false clears, all controls,
and <=8/20 negative escalations. Failure ends tuning: no rescue draw. Abstention
on positives escalates; all-abstain fails the negative burden check.

If earned and passed, at most two finalist checkpoints consume 48 Luna judgments.
Strong confirmation of decision-changing leads and four random Luna clears share
the 20-strong special pool with A/A, order controls and diagnostics. Two checkpoints
with no additional verified change stop recurring work. No deployment reliability
or 99% claim. Even 0/20 independent misses has a one-sided 95% bound of 13.91%;
related sources cannot be described as independent deployment trials.

Hard caps: 220 strong attempts (110 each provider), 150 Luna logical-item attempts.
Bundling is charged by every item; failures/retries are charged too. Six root
pre-consensus Sol Ultra inspections are conservatively debited to special: 214
strong remaining, 14 special remaining, 48 final reserved. They are invalid
reference judgments; unknown historical time/usage stays null. Only actual
completion latency drives projections, with 2x reserve and shared Sol/Luna quota.
One request/provider, at most two after ten clean completions. No substitutions:
Opus 5.5 high via Opus; Sol 6.1 xhigh via root; GPT-6 Luna medium subscription
shadow/triage only. Root must reconcile unknown historical elapsed time (or supply a conservative bound) before opening a phase. A/A and original/reversed A/B utility controls have a receipt API requiring three actual charged special judgments per strong grader; a contradiction stops that grader's automatic utility use. The pure budget controller dispatches nothing.

Elapsed panel ceilings: pilot 2 h including extension/adjudication; conditional
work 1 h; optional checkpoints 30 min; final 2 h reserved after Sunday freeze;
total 5.5 h. The final one-shot is <=20 licensed v2 event cards + four controls,
dual strong once (48 calls), no Luna required, no prettier-result retry/retuning.
Full local suite remains independent. Final claims require zero recovered
protected truth/no grounded confirmed leak plus operating constraints and stated
coverage/GeoCLIP precision limits. A nonzero development improvement is not a
final pass. Utility acceptance remains with the owner.

## Local entry points

`python -m bench.vision_panel request OUT/pilot-request.json` writes a missing-input
skeleton. `budget-init OUT/grading-state.json` writes conservative counters only.
`validate PLAN` checks provenance and guards; `seal PLAN PUBLIC PRIVATE` copies
only root-supplied processed PNG cards once qualified inputs exist. `verify-card
CARD` checks one public attachment. `reveal PUBLIC PRIVATE ROUND MAP_SHA256`
requires all first-pass seals. `scoring.begin/finish`, local decision records and
budget transitions are Python APIs for root orchestration, not dispatch tools.
Keep generated artifacts/answers in ignored out/ or .tmp. The single full gate
remains root-scheduled and HELD; affected synthetic tests use canonical SH only.

Synthetic tests use real files, hashes, exclusive-write and seal validation, while stubbing the fsync system boundary to avoid repeated shared-host disk flushes. They do not establish power-loss durability or actual model/image capability.
