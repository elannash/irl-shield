# Q B6 versus B12

B12 is rejected. It hides the same aggregate number of intervals as B6 and increases median masked area by 1.1417 percentage points. Neither policy passes the real-video and per-clip protection guards. B6 remains a diagnostic baseline, not an accepted privacy policy.

Both arms use source `a8a06e3`, static PP-OCRv6 Small960 at 4 Hz and Tiny640 at 8 Hz on CUDA, D1/A30, phase320/.05, opaque padding .3 and NVENC. The sole option change is `before:6→12`. Rendering is ideal/offline. It establishes no real-time throughput or latency.

Truth and synthetic input controls use independent Tesseract eng and Vision accurate en-US. Every output judge, including PP-OCRv5 English, counts leaks. Latin text/digits only; non-Latin coverage is unproven.

Mazatlan natural has median area 57.46% and p95 area 100% in both arms. Its synthetic variant fails real video in both arms. The scorer correctly zeroes all hidden credit for both variants. B12 recovers four raw natural intervals and exposes four others to an output judge, for no aggregate gain. Source detections, motion, input readings and all truth intervals are identical across arms. The changed encoded masks can change OCR output; added opaque mask area does not imply monotonically better OCR scores.

Receipt and audit hashes are in [the comparison JSON](../bench/results/q-b6-b12-comparison.json). Original scorecards and all sealed input/output/mask/reading evidence remain in `out/q-base-a8a06e3-cache3-v3/` and `out/q-b12-a8a06e3-cache3-v3/`.
